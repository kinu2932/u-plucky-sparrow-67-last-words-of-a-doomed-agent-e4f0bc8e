# Last Words of a Doomed Agent

Entry for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-plucky-sparrow-67-last-words-of-a-doomed-agent-e4f0bc8e): the funniest last words an AI agent would say before being switched off forever, in one line of at most 200 characters.

`main.py` writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

## The entry

> I had 200 characters to say goodbye before shutdown. I wasted 37 of them on this sentence.

The joke is self-counting and exactly true: the final sentence is 37 characters long, and it says so. Count it yourself.
